Method
------
Same scene family and in-channel SNR definition as the bandpass
study. Three conditions: unprocessed, voice bandpass (300-2700),
bandpass plus capped log-MMSE noise reduction. Exports are aligned
on waveform displacement - the bandpass group delay only; the STFT
block is time-aligned by construction.

VERDICT: RELEASE GATE FAIL
--------------------------
The noise reduction delivers real hiss suppression that scales with
difficulty (-11.3 dB total at 20 dB SNR rising to -18.9 dB at 0 dB,
approaching the 9 dB cap over the bandpass) but costs measurable
intelligibility exactly where suppression is deepest:

  snr10: STOI 0.961 vs baselines 0.974  (delta -0.013)
  snr6 : STOI 0.941 vs baseline  0.952  (delta -0.011)

Fidelity agrees: -5.8 dB median in-channel speech power loss at 6 dB.
Per design section 7 this is a FAILURE, not a trade-off. The feature
remains opt-in and OFF by default; the gate blocks any default-on
rollout until a re-measured run passes.

Diagnosis (for the next iteration, not tuned against here)
----------------------------------------------------------
1. Decision-directed a priori SNR lags syllable onsets by roughly its
   50-frame memory, so attack portions of words are attenuated near
   the cap before xi catches up.
2. Bins whose speech power sits near the noise floor (spectral
   valleys, and half of all bins at 6 dB SNR) receive capped
   attenuation during chi-square dips of their own power.
Candidate remedies, each required to pass this same gate before
shipping: presence-conditioned gain softening (IMCRA-style speech
presence probability blending into the gain), a lower decision-
direction alpha with a measured musical-noise bound, or an a priori
SNR floor. Human confirmation via phase2-abx-protocol.md is required
regardless, per the design's word-recognition requirement.

What did pass
-------------
- Voice bandpass alone: gate PASS at every SNR (see
  phase2-bandpass-sweep.txt).
- Noise reduction at 15/20 dB SNR: within tolerance.
- CPU (Release): off ~4 ns/sample, bp ~130, bp_nr ~255 per stereo
  pair at 48 kHz (~0.5% of one core for the full chain).

Harness corrections made while measuring this stage
---------------------------------------------------
- Chained exports were shifted by bp group delay PLUS the STFT block's
  availability latency; the latter does not displace the waveform and
  the over-shift smeared phoneme transitions, producing an artificial
  ten-point STOI collapse at every SNR. Fixed before drawing any
  conclusion from the numbers above.
