AM voice bandpass evaluation - upper cutoff study (Phase 2a)
================================================================
Date: 2026-08-22
Plan: implementation-plan-phase2.md task P2-7
Build: Release (-O3), af_sweep harness target

Method
------
Four deterministic formant-speech scenes (20 s, four transmissions of
four words each). snrDb targets the noise power INSIDE the transmitted
300-3400 Hz channel per ETSI EN 300 086; hiss itself is wideband.
Conditions: unprocessed, and the voice bandpass (300 Hz lower edge,
200 Hz transitions) at 2500 / 2700 / 3000 Hz upper cutoffs. All exported
audio is time-aligned on each condition's reported group delay before
correlation scoring; fidelity is delay-compensated the same way.

Columns: median power reduction in noise-only gaps (noiseReddB), median
in-channel speech power change against ground truth (fidelitydB),
largest single-sample step (maxJump), CPU cost per stereo pair.

Findings
--------
1. Cleanliness: about 10 dB of wideband hiss removed at every cutoff,
   matching the passband-to-bandwidth ratio. Transient jump metric
   drops 3-7x versus unprocessed.
2. Intelligibility: every variant stays within the 0.01 gate of the
   unprocessed baseline at every tested SNR. Ordering at 6 dB:
   bp_3000 0.9519, bp_2700 0.9510, bp_2500 0.9433 vs baseline 0.9516.
   2500 costs a measurable margin; 2700 and 3000 are indistinguishable
   from it and from each other within 0.002.
3. Default 2700 Hz justified. 3000 Hz buys nothing measurable over it;
   2500 Hz spends intelligibility margin without additional cleanliness.
4. CPU: ~125 ns per stereo pair (~0.6% of one core at 48 kHz). This is
   above the plan's 50 ns estimate because the 913-tap kernel runs at
   full SIMD efficiency (~15 GFLOP/s); absolute cost remains negligible
   and the budget line is revised here rather than silently ignored.

Methodology corrections made during measurement (per contract rule 2):
- Generator shapes clean audio to the EN 300 086 channel and defines
  scene SNR in-channel; wideband-only definitions inflated baselines.
- Unvoiced broadband phonemes added so correlation metrics see spectral
  spread comparable to real speech.
- Exports align on reported block delays; an earlier misalignment pinned
  bandpass scores at a constant 0.81 regardless of SNR.
